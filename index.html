How to get your CSV

Most banksOnline banking, open your account, look for Download, Export or Statements, and pick CSV. Twelve months gives the best results.
Credit cardsCard issuers usually have Activity, then Download. Subscriptions often live on a card, so check it too.
Any formatComma, semicolon or tab. US and European dates and numbers. One amount column, or separate debit and credit columns.
